# Crate Dig 1.0.9 — brag plan

**What it is:** Crate Dig is a desktop project organizer for music makers (Windows + Mac). It turns your music folders into a library you can dig through: projects, versions and samples in one view, My Crates, and Crate Roulette to pick what's next.

**Who it's for:** producers with drives full of half-finished sessions ("Final_final_v9", "3AM Bad Decisions"). They get back to ideas they forgot about.

**What's new in 1.0.9 (the focus):**
- Remembers **multiple music folders**. **Add folder** appends and **Browse** replaces.
- Opens **saved indexes without rescanning at every launch**. **Check for crate updates** compares files and lets you choose what to scan.
- Also: Mac rendering pauses while hidden, and there are separate "Don't show again" preferences (left out of the video).

**What sets it apart:** it's built around crate digging (vinyl, crates, roulette), it never moves or renames your files, and it's one purchase with no subscription.

**Funniest real material:** the site's own intro reel. It spins through "Banger #67", "2021 Nostalgia", "Final_final_v9" and "3AM Bad Decisions", then lands on "Your Next Hit — YOU HAD IT ALL ALONG."

**Visual hook:** that roulette reel with its orange needle, which then throws its vinyl into the next scene.

**Share caption:** Crate Dig 1.0.9 remembers every music folder you add and stops rescanning at every launch.

Start with the joke every producer knows: a reel of your own badly named project files. Then show the app, then the two 1.0.9 changes as one continuous product moment with the same window and the same Library Sources panel. Finish on Crate Roulette doing its thing, and the price.

## Sound
Lo-fi boom-bap in A minor at 90 BPM (a nod to the demo's `heartless_89_A.wav`). Parts: Rhodes, sub bass, swung hats and vinyl crackle. The hook is lowpassed and opens up on the drop at the reveal. Every SFX is tuned to A minor (reel ticks, landing bell, folder plucks, update shimmer, roulette ticks) and runs through the same room reverb as the music, sitting under it.
